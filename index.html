PackKaro

Crop labels, sort items, pack with zero mistakes.

Step 1: Upload your label PDFs

Choose a PDF, then press + to add more files. Nothing is uploaded to any server - the work happens inside your own browser. Pages we are not sure about go to Manual Check.

Starting…

Use this step to customise your labels with your company name and logo. They are printed on every label, so your brand is seen on every parcel.

Step 2: Crop and make print-ready labels

Scan your PDFs in Step 1 first. Each label is cropped, sorted by item code, numbered, and marked * for quantity 1 or ** for quantity above 1. Use English letters and numbers for the shop name and message.

Use 100 if your label fills the page. If the label sits on the top half of the page, use 50.
