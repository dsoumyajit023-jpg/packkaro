PackKaro

Crop labels, sort items, pack with zero mistakes.

Step 1: Where do your orders come from?

Type your order source. Pick a suggestion or type your own name. When you fill a box, a new one opens automatically.

Step 2: Upload your label PDFs

Pick one or many PDF files. Nothing is uploaded to any server - the work happens inside your own browser. Pages we are not sure about go to Manual Check.

Step 3: Crop and make print-ready labels

Scan your PDFs in Step 2 first. Each label is cropped, sorted by item code, numbered, and marked * for quantity 1 or ** for quantity above 1. Use English letters and numbers for the shop name and message.

Use 100 if your label fills the page. If the label sits on the top half of the page, use 50.
